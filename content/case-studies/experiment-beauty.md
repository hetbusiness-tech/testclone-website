# When a Better Product Still Loses

---

## 01 — The Brand

Experiment Beauty had already done the difficult part.

The formulations were strong, the ingredients were carefully chosen, and customers who tried the products often became loyal believers.

The problem was happening somewhere else: despite having a product the brand believed could compete with — and outperform — alternatives in the market, **a competitor with a noticeably weaker product was consistently outselling them.**

> The product wasn't the problem. The digital experience around it was.

---

## 02 — The Challenge

**A better product was losing because the store made buying harder.**

The competitor's website was simply easier to use:
- It loaded faster.
- Its product pages were cleaner.
- Its checkout required fewer steps.

Meanwhile, Experiment Beauty's Shopify store was creating friction at several critical points in the customer journey:
- Mobile pages were slower to load
- Product pages felt cluttered
- The checkout experience demanded more effort than necessary

The result was a frustrating gap:

> **Better Product** &rarr; **More Friction** &rarr; **More Hesitation** &rarr; **Lost Sales**

![The Friction-Heavy Experience — Slow loading, cluttered pages, and excessive mobile steps losing sales](/portfolio/exp-beauty-5.png)

The brand didn't necessarily need more traffic. **It needed to stop losing the traffic it already had.**

---

## 03 — The Diagnosis

**The competitive gap wasn't the product. It was execution.**

Technostripe looked at the store through the same journey a potential customer would experience — from landing on a product page to completing a purchase. Three primary friction areas stood out:

- **Site Performance**  
  Slow loading, particularly on mobile, was creating unnecessary waiting before customers could even engage with the store.
- **Product Page Experience**  
  Important product information was competing with a cluttered layout, making the path from discovering the product to understanding its value less direct.
- **Checkout Friction**  
  The checkout experience contained more steps and interruptions than necessary.

The product already had a reason to be chosen. The store needed to make that decision effortless.

---

## 04 — The Strategy

**Make the quality of the product visible through the quality of the experience.**

Technostripe approached the rebuild around one principle: **when the product is strong, the store shouldn't get in its way.**

Rather than changing the product or reinventing the brand, we focused on removing the friction surrounding the purchase:

- **Speed** &rarr; Make the store faster, especially on mobile
- **Clarity** &rarr; Make product pages easier to understand and navigate
- **Focus** &rarr; Remove unnecessary visual distractions
- **Simplicity** &rarr; Reduce friction throughout checkout

> The objective wasn't to make the store more complicated. **It was to make buying feel effortless.**

---

## 05 — What We Changed

### 01. Store Performance Optimisation
We focused on improving the store's loading experience so customers could move through the website without unnecessary delays. Particular attention was given to mobile performance, where slow loading can interrupt the shopping journey before it even begins.  
*Less waiting. More shopping.*

### 02. Cleaner Product Pages
Product pages were redesigned around a clearer hierarchy, giving the formulation, ingredients, and product benefits more room to communicate. Visual clutter was reduced so customers could focus on understanding the product rather than navigating around the page.  
*The product became the focal point.*

![High-Converting PDP Layout — Formulation breakdown, verified social proof, and seamless Add to Cart](/portfolio/exp-beauty-2.png)

### 03. Improved Mobile Experience
The shopping experience was refined around the reality of mobile-first beauty shopping. Layouts, content, and interactions were structured to make browsing and product discovery intuitive on smaller screens.  
*A seamless experience, wherever the customer was shopping.*

### 04. Streamlined Checkout
The checkout journey was tightened to remove unnecessary friction and reduce the number of moments where a ready-to-buy customer could hesitate or drop away.

> **The goal was simple:** `Product Page` &rarr; `Checkout` &rarr; `Purchase` with as little resistance as possible.

![Streamlined Mobile Checkout — Accelerated Information > Shipping > Payment pipeline](/portfolio/exp-beauty-3.png)

**A strong product still needs a strong buying experience.**

In competitive beauty markets, customers aren't comparing products in isolation. They're comparing the entire experience:
- How quickly the website loads
- How easily they can understand the product
- How confidently they can make a decision
- How much effort it takes to complete the purchase

Experiment Beauty didn't need to become a different brand to compete. **It needed its digital experience to compete at the same level as its product.**
