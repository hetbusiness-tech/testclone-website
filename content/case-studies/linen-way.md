# Surviving the Biggest Day of the Year

---

## 01 — The Brand

Linen Way is a home lifestyle brand built around everyday essentials — from napkins and towels to bedding and accessories.

Like many D2C brands, some moments matter more than others. A major seasonal sale was one of those moments. And this time, the brand had something else working in its favour: **one of its products had started gaining significant organic attention.**

The traffic was coming. **The store just wasn't ready for it.**

---

## 02 — The Challenge

**The biggest opportunity became the biggest risk.**

When the seasonal campaign went live, traffic surged exactly as expected. But instead of turning that attention into sales, the store began struggling under the load:
- Checkout errors started appearing.
- Products that had already sold out were still being shown as available.
- Orders were coming in for inventory that no longer existed.

What should have been the brand's biggest selling day quickly became a scramble of:

> **Failed checkouts** &rarr; **Oversold products** &rarr; **Cancellations** &rarr; **Refunds** &rarr; **Frustrated customers**

![Store Under Pressure — Checkout errors, oversold inventory, and cancellations during traffic spikes](/portfolio/linen-2.png)

The problem wasn't a lack of demand. **The store couldn't reliably handle the demand it had created.**

---

## 03 — The Diagnosis

**The store worked on an average day. The problem was the day that mattered.**

The existing technical setup had been built around normal traffic conditions. But major campaigns don't create normal conditions. We identified two critical areas that needed urgent attention:

- **Performance Under Traffic Spikes**  
  Theme code, app dependencies, and the wider storefront setup needed to be optimised for sudden, concurrent increases in visitors and activity.
- **Inventory Reliability & Race Conditions**  
  Stock availability needed to remain strictly accurate while large numbers of customers were browsing, adding products to cart, and checking out simultaneously.

The goal wasn't simply to make the website faster. **It was to make the store resilient under pressure.**

![The Concurrency Bottleneck — Traffic spikes overwhelming unoptimized theme scripts and checkout API](/portfolio/linen-3.png)

---

## 04 — The Strategy

**Build for the spike, not the average.**

Technostripe approached the project from one simple principle: **a Shopify store isn't truly ready if it only works when traffic is predictable.**

We focused on strengthening the store's technical foundation so that a sudden surge in demand wouldn't turn into a technical failure:

- **Performance** &rarr; Reduce unnecessary technical overhead
- **Scalability** &rarr; Prepare the storefront for sudden traffic surges
- **Inventory Accuracy** &rarr; Prevent ghost orders for unavailable products
- **Checkout Reliability** &rarr; Make the purchase journey dependable when demand peaks

> **The objective was straightforward:** When the next big moment arrived, the store needed to stay standing.

---

## 05 — What We Changed

### 01. Storefront Performance Optimisation
We worked through the storefront's technical foundation to reduce unnecessary load and improve how the Shopify store handled high concurrency. The focus was not on chasing vanity speed scores — it was about keeping the shopping experience stable when traffic suddenly multiplied.

### 02. Theme & App Optimisation
Theme code and app-related dependencies were audited and trimmed to remove blocking third-party scripts. Elements that performed adequately under normal conditions were refactored to survive extreme surges.

### 03. Inventory Safeguards
We strengthened the inventory synchronization so product availability remained aligned in real time with actual stock levels, preventing the costly issue of overselling unavailable items.

### 04. Checkout Reliability
The purchase journey was hardened with high-traffic conditions in mind, eliminating the risk of technical drop-offs at the checkout gate.
