## The Challenge

Miniclasix, a baby clothing brand, came to Technostripe convinced their ad account was the problem. Spend was steady, the creative looked polished, and the brand had real emotional appeal — soft, minimal, the kind of product that photographs beautifully. But sales weren't scaling with spend, and every attempt to push budget higher just made the inefficiency more expensive.

Where things stood before:

- CPC around $1.40, reasonable for the category but climbing month over month
- CTR at 0.9%, decent for cold traffic but weak on retargeting, where it should have been much higher
- CAC sitting at $52 against an average order value of $58 — essentially breaking even before accounting for returns and ad platform fees
- ROAS stuck at 1.6x, nowhere near sustainable for a brand trying to reinvest into growth
- Frequency on core audiences pushing past 5.1, with CTR visibly declining week over week — a clear fatigue curve nobody had caught in time

## The Solution

The account had been running one broad campaign trying to speak to every kind of buyer at once — new parents, gift-givers, repeat customers — with a single generic message. We split the funnel properly: cold traffic built around relatable new-parent moments, warm retargeting built around size guides and fabric quality (the actual objections holding people back), and a dedicated flow for gifting occasions like baby showers, which had been getting no specific attention at all despite being a meaningfully different buyer.

While auditing performance by landing page, we noticed something the ad metrics alone wouldn't have caught: a large share of clicks were landing on a sizing page with confusing, inconsistent measurements across product types — a likely reason retargeted, high-intent traffic was still bouncing before purchase. That sent us back into the Shopify store, where we standardized the sizing information into one clear, consistent format across the catalog and added it directly to product pages instead of a separate help doc. It was a small fix on the surface, but it addressed a real point of hesitation for parents buying sizes they couldn't try on first.

## The Outcome

Where things landed:

- CPC down to $0.95
- CTR up to 2.3% on retargeting specifically
- CAC reduced to $24
- ROAS climbing to 5.7x
- Frequency brought back under 2.8 through structured creative rotation across the three separated funnels

## The Takeaway

What looked like an ads problem turned out to be two smaller problems layered on top of each other — one in targeting, one in trust. Solving only the ads side would have left real money on the table. The most efficient audit doesn't stop at the ad account; it follows the traffic all the way to where it actually stops converting.
