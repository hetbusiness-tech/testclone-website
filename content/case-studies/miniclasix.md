# Unlocking Growth by Splitting the Funnel and Fixing Fit

---

## 01 — The Brand

Miniclasix is a premium baby clothing brand creating thoughtfully designed apparel for newborns and infants — combining super-soft fabrics with timeless European-inspired styling.

On paper, the brand had everything needed for rapid e-commerce expansion:
- Adorable, thumb-stopping product photography
- Strong emotional brand appeal
- A steady advertising budget on Meta (Facebook & Instagram)

Yet despite steady ad spend and healthy impression numbers, customer acquisition was stagnating and margins were thin.

> **The ad account looked healthy on the surface. But a hidden audience mismatch and sizing anxiety were quietly killing sales.**

---

## 02 — The Challenge

**The creative was polished, but customer acquisition was costing more than the clothes were making.**

Before Technostripe stepped in, Miniclasix was facing an uphill battle:

- **CPC — $1.40**  
  Click costs were climbing as the account exhausted narrow interest groups.
- **CTR — 0.9%**  
  Average engagement because ads were delivered generically to anyone interested in "baby."
- **CAC — $52**  
  Far too high against a $68 average order value.
- **ROAS — 1.6x**  
  Barely breaking even after manufacturing, packaging, and shipping costs.

The team was treating every shopper like the same person. In reality, **a frantic expectant mother shops very differently from a grandmother buying a luxury baby shower gift.**

![A Funnel That Wasn't Working — Spend was steady, creative looked polished, but sales stalled](/portfolio/miniclasix-2.png)

---

## 03 — The Diagnosis

**Two major bottlenecks were choking profitability: audience blending and sizing hesitation.**

When Technostripe audited the account and the on-site behavior:

- **The Blended Audience Trap**  
  Ad sets lumped pregnant mothers, new parents, grandparents, and gift shoppers into the same campaigns with identical messaging and landing pages.
- **The Sizing Anxiety Gap**  
  Baby clothes sizes (0-3m, 3-6m, 6-12m) vary wildly between brands. Shoppers loved the outfits, but abandoned carts because they couldn't confidently determine whether their baby would fit or outgrow the piece within weeks.
- **Post-Purchase Returns**  
  Customers who did buy frequently returned items due to sizing surprises, eroding operational profit.

---

## 04 — The Strategy

**Segment the audience by intent, and eliminate fit hesitation on the product page.**

Technostripe applied a two-pronged solution:

1. **Funnel Segmentation:** Split campaigns into 3 distinct buyer personas with custom messaging:
   - **Expecting & New Parents:** Focus on softness, organic hypoallergenic fabric, and easy diaper-change snaps.
   - **Gift Shoppers & Family:** Focus on premium gift boxes, bundles, and "ready-to-gift" unboxing experiences.
   - **Repeat Customers:** Showcase seasonal drops and size-up collections.

2. **Zero-Hesitation Sizing Chart:** Re-engineer the sizing guide on Shopify to include baby weight (lbs/kg) and length (inches/cm), rather than just vague age months.

---

## 05 — What We Changed

### 01. Tri-Split Audience Funnel
We restructured Meta campaigns to separate New Parents, Gift Shoppers, and Retention audiences, tailoring creative hooks and landing destinations for each group.

### 02. Interactive Baby Sizing Guide
We replaced the static text sizing table with a visual, interactive size recommender based on age, height, and weight, giving parents total confidence before ordering.

![Visual Sizing Guide — Detailed height, weight, and fit guidance eliminating cart hesitation](/portfolio/miniclasix-4.png)

### 03. Gifting Experience on Shopify
For the gift-buyer segment, we introduced 1-click gift box add-ons, personalized gift notes at checkout, and direct-to-recipient shipping with hidden pricing.

### 04. Creative Restructure in Ads Manager
We introduced UGC reels from real parents demonstrating the soft fabric stretch and effortless snap buttons, dramatically lifting scroll-stopping engagement.
