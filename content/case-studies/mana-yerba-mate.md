# When Success Starts Breaking the Business

---

## 01 — The Brand

Mana Yerba Mate is a fast-growing beverage brand that had reached the milestone every founder dreams of: **demand was accelerating.**

Orders were climbing week after week, taking the business from a manageable direct-to-consumer operation into a high-velocity beverage company.

But growth radically changes the demands placed on a business. Processes that worked smoothly at 30 orders a day began buckling when volume scaled into the hundreds daily.

> **The product was scaling. The operational systems behind it were not.**

---

## 02 — The Challenge

**Growth was creating operational bottlenecks faster than the team could solve them.**

As order volume increased, cracks began appearing across every post-purchase customer touchpoint:

- **Inventory Drift**  
  Stock counts in Shopify were drifting away from actual physical warehouse inventory.
- **Fulfillment Backlogs**  
  Orders were delayed in the manual processing queue, turning standard delivery promises into angry emails and chargebacks.
- **Support Team Overwhelm**  
  Customer service was handled manually, making it impossible to answer "Where is my order?" inquiries in real time.

```
More Orders → Operational Pressure → Delayed Shipments → Customer Friction → Margin Erosion
```

The business wasn't struggling because demand had stalled. **It was struggling because its operational engine couldn't keep pace with its success.**

![Fulfillment Delays — Growing order queue piling up with unfulfilled statuses and delivery lags](/portfolio/mana-2.png)

---

## 03 — The Diagnosis

**The problem wasn't sales. It was everything that happened after the sale.**

The Shopify storefront was generating clicks and checkouts efficiently, but nearly every backend step still required human intervention:

- **Disconnected Stock Sync**  
  Warehouse counts weren't updating the storefront in real time, creating ghost orders for sold-out flavours.
- **Manual Fulfillment Bottlenecks**  
  Warehouse pick-and-pack slips required manual export and tag management, dragging fulfillment lead times from hours to days.
- **Reactive Customer Communications**  
  Tracking numbers and shipment milestone notifications weren't reliably triggered, flooding customer support.

![The Stockout Dilemma — Disconnected warehouse counts leading to inventory drift and overselling](/portfolio/mana-3.png)

## 05 — What We Changed

### 01. Real-Time Inventory Synchronisation
We integrated bidirectional inventory syncing between Shopify and warehouse management systems. Stock quantities now update within milliseconds of every order or stock restock.

### 02. Automated Fulfillment Triggers
Orders are automatically validated, tagged, fraud-checked, and dispatched to the fulfillment floor instantly upon payment capture — eliminating hours of manual triage.

![Automated Inventory Synchronization — Real-time stock levels and automated fulfillment triggers](/portfolio/mana-4.png)

### 03. High-Velocity Order Routing
Order processing logic routes orders based on warehouse proximity and shipping priority, drastically cutting down delivery transit windows.

### 04. Proactive Customer Communication
Automated event-based shipping updates proactively notify customers at every stage (order confirmed, packed, out for delivery, delivered), eliminating 70% of inbound support tickets.
