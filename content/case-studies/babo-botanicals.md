## The Challenge

Babo Botanicals brings plant-based skincare and mineral sun protection to the whole family. The storefront needed to make product education feel simple, approachable, and useful while helping shoppers quickly find the right protection for their needs.

## The Solution

We organized the experience around clear product categories, easy-to-scan benefits, and confident paths into sunscreen, baby care, bath, and body collections. The visual language keeps the botanical story warm while making product choices straightforward on mobile.

## The Outcome

The result is a bright, accessible shopping experience that turns ingredient trust and everyday protection into a clear path to purchase.

## The Takeaway

Skincare commerce works best when education removes hesitation instead of adding another layer of complexity.
