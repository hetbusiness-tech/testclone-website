## The Challenge

Kayaa Skincare, a dermatology-focused skincare brand, came to Technostripe with a harder problem than most: their product actually worked, backed by real formulation and clinical-style claims, but paid ads weren't converting nearly as well as the product deserved. Skincare is a crowded, skeptical category — customers have been burned by empty promises before, and ad creative that doesn't earn trust fast gets scrolled past without a second thought.

Where things stood before:

- CPC around $2.10, high even for a competitive category
- CTR at 0.7%, a sign the creative wasn't stopping the scroll
- CAC at $61 against an average order value of $54 — losing money on every first-time customer before any repeat purchase
- ROAS sitting at 1.4x, well below sustainable
- Frequency climbing past 4.6 on the core retargeting audience, with engagement dropping noticeably after the second week of any given creative

## The Solution

The existing ads leaned entirely on product shots and generic before/after claims — the same approach every competitor in the category was already using, which meant the brand's actual clinical credibility wasn't cutting through. We rebuilt the creative strategy around proof and specificity: ingredient-focused messaging, real usage timelines ("here's what changes by week 2, week 4, week 8"), and a review-driven retargeting sequence that surfaced real customer results instead of stock-style claims. Cold and warm audiences were also split apart, since the skepticism a first-time visitor needed addressed was very different from what a warm, already-interested visitor needed to see.

During the audit, the data pointed to a conversion drop-off that had nothing to do with the ads themselves — a meaningful share of retargeted visitors were reaching the product page, reading the ingredient list, and leaving without adding to cart. That sent us into the Shopify store, where we found the ingredient and usage information was buried below the fold, under a separate tab most visitors never clicked. We restructured the product page to surface that information immediately, alongside the ad-driven proof points, so the trust the ads were building didn't get lost the moment someone landed on the site.

## The Outcome

Where things landed:

- CPC down to $1.25
- CTR up to 2.1%
- CAC reduced to $26
- ROAS climbing to 6.1x
- Frequency held under 2.6 by rotating proof angles (ingredients, timelines, reviews) instead of repeating one message

## The Takeaway

For a skepticism-heavy category like derma skincare, the win wasn't a cleverer ad — it was making sure the trust built in the ad didn't have to be rebuilt from scratch the moment someone landed on the site. Ad performance and site experience have to tell the same story; when they don't, the gap between them is exactly where conversions leak out.
