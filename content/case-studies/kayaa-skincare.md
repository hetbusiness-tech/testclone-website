# Turning Skepticism Into Sales When Great Products Aren't Trusted Fast Enough

---

## 01 — The Brand

Kayaa Skincare is a dermatology-backed skincare brand built around clinical-grade formulations, active ingredients, and dermatologically tested efficacy.

The brand had established something many cosmetics businesses struggle for years to achieve: **genuine product credibility.**

The formulations worked. The ingredients were ethically sourced and rigorously tested. Yet the business was stuck on an acquisition plateau:
- Paid ad traffic was arriving, but visitors weren't converting.
- Cold audiences scrolled past ads without engaging.
- Acquisition costs were dangerously high.

> **The product had real scientific substance. The marketing and storefront simply weren't communicating proof fast enough.**

---

## 02 — The Challenge

**Generic skincare promises in a hyper-skeptical market.**

Before Technostripe stepped in, account economics were bleeding:

- **CPC — $2.10**  
  Click costs were steep as ads struggled to stand out in the crowded beauty space.
- **CTR — 0.7%**  
  Shoppers saw the ads as "just another skincare product."
- **CAC — $61**  
  Higher than the $54 average order value — meaning the brand lost money on every new customer.
- **ROAS — 1.4x**  
  Barely above break-even, preventing any aggressive scale.
- **Frequency — 4.6+**  
  Ad fatigue caused performance to collapse within 10 to 14 days of launching a creative.

The ads looked like every other beauty brand: static bottle shot, exaggerated before/after promise, generic "glow" claim.

![The Skepticism Barrier — Generic claims and high frequency leading to ad fatigue and $61 CAC](/portfolio/kayaa-3.png)

---

## 03 — The Diagnosis

**The problem wasn't the formulation. It was the lack of visible proof.**

Skincare shoppers are the most skeptical consumers on the internet. They have been burned by false promises and filtered before-and-afters.

Technostripe audited the entire funnel and found two major friction points:

1. **Vague Claims in Advertising:** Ads highlighted generic lifestyle imagery rather than showcasing active percentage concentrations and dermatological evidence.
2. **Hidden Information on Shopify:** When interested shoppers landed on the product detail page, critical ingredient breakdowns and clinical trial results were hidden deep inside collapsed accordion tabs.

> **The ad sparked interest, but the product page forced shoppers to do homework to verify the claims.**

---

## 04 — The Strategy

**Stop making vague claims. Start showing verifiable proof.**

Technostripe rebuilt the acquisition funnel around one rule: **in a skeptical market, specificity creates trust.**

We structured the customer journey around four pillars:
- **Active Ingredient Transparency:** Spotlight exact percentages (e.g. Niacinamide, Hyaluronic Acid, SPF 50 filters).
- **Realistic Timeline Expectations:** Replace "overnight miracle" tropes with clear 2-week, 4-week, and 8-week expectation roadmaps.
- **Dermatologist & Review Validation:** Deploy verified clinical testimonials in retargeting.
- **On-Page Proof Alignment:** Surface ingredients, clinical trial results, and dermatologist endorsements immediately above the fold on the PDP.
