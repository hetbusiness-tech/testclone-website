# A Shopify Store Designed to Let the Jewellery Speak

---

## 01 — The Brand

MATILDA creates meaningful jewellery built around simplicity, intention, and everyday expression.

Born and handcrafted in Portugal, the brand focuses on jewellery with an understated aesthetic, produced largely through local craftsmanship. Its range spans earrings, necklaces, rings, bracelets, and anklets, alongside personalisation and bespoke gifting experiences.

The opportunity wasn't simply to make MATILDA look more premium — **it was to make the digital experience feel as considered as the jewellery itself.**

---

## 02 — The Challenge

**The product had the story. The store was getting in the way.**

The existing shopping experience suffered from critical friction points:

- **01 — Product pages felt generic**  
  The presentation didn't give individual pieces enough space to communicate their character, details, and story.
- **02 — Too much visual competition**  
  Heavy layouts and unnecessary visual noise made it harder for the delicate jewellery imagery to take centre stage.
- **03 — The buying journey lacked clarity**  
  Information wasn't presented at the moments customers needed it, creating hesitation during consideration.
- **04 — Checkout friction**  
  Customers who had already decided to buy faced unnecessary barriers and steps at the final stage.

> **The core problem:** MATILDA didn't need more noise. It needed a better environment for its products to sell.

![The Cluttered Experience — Heavy layouts and generic presentation obscuring the craft](/portfolio/matilda-2.png)

---

## 03 — The Strategy

**Less clutter. More intention.**

Technostripe rebuilt the Shopify experience around one guiding principle: **let the jewellery lead.**

Instead of adding decorative elements to communicate value, we simplified the experience around what mattered most:

> **Product** &rarr; **Story** &rarr; **Confidence** &rarr; **Purchase**

This meant rethinking how customers discovered pieces, how individual items were showcased, and how smoothly they moved from consideration to checkout.

---

## 04 — What We Changed

### 01. An Editorial Shopping Experience
We moved away from a dense, generic e-commerce grid to create a cleaner visual hierarchy where high-resolution product photography could do the storytelling.

### 02. Product Pages Built Around Detail
Each piece received room to breathe. We reworked PDP layouts to highlight craftsmanship details, metal specifications, and styling inspiration without burying essential purchasing actions.

![Refined Product Discovery — Clear category hierarchy with shipping and warranty trust signals](/portfolio/matilda-3.png)

### 03. Intuitive Catalogue Discovery
With a broad catalogue spanning earrings, necklaces, rings, and curated gift edits, navigation was redesigned to help customers find the right piece effortlessly by style, occasion, or material.

![Editorial Category Browsing — Thoughtful presentation allowing jewellery details to shine](/portfolio/matilda-4.png)

### 04. Seamless Express Checkout
We eliminated final-step friction by introducing 1-click Express Checkout, clear shipping timelines, and transparent return policies right alongside purchase buttons.

![Express Checkout Integration — Removing hesitation between product choice and confirmed order](/portfolio/matilda-5.png)
