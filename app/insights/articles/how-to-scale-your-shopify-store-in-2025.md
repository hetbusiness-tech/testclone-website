## The ₹1 Crore Ceiling — And How to Break Through It

Most DTC brands in India hit a wall around **₹10–20 lakhs/month**. The tactics that got you here — decent ads, basic Shopify theme, word-of-mouth — simply stop working at scale. To cross ₹1 Crore/month, you need a different operating system entirely.

Here's the exact framework we use at Technostripe with our Shopify Plus clients.

---

## 1. Move to Shopify Plus (If You Haven't Already)

Standard Shopify caps out at 100 themes, can't handle checkout customisations, and charges higher transaction fees. At scale, these bottlenecks cost you real money.

**What Shopify Plus unlocks:**
- Custom checkout with upsells, cross-sells, and trust builders
- Shopify Scripts for tiered pricing and bundle logic
- Launchpad for scheduled sales events
- Dedicated Merchant Success Manager
- Lower transaction fees (saves ₹5–8 lakhs/year at scale)

> The migration pays for itself within 2–3 months for any store doing ₹30L+/month.

---

## 2. Fix Your Core Web Vitals First

No amount of ad spend compensates for a slow website. Google PageSpeed and mobile performance directly impact your Meta Ads quality score and Google Shopping rankings.

**Target benchmarks:**
- LCP (Largest Contentful Paint): **< 2.5s**
- FID / INP: **< 200ms**
- CLS: **< 0.1**
- Mobile PageSpeed Score: **> 70**

At Technostripe, we've seen 18–34% ROAS improvements purely from speed optimisation on Shopify stores.

---

## 3. Build Your Performance Marketing Engine

At ₹1 Crore/month, you need **Meta Ads + Google Ads running simultaneously**, not just one or the other.

### Meta Ads Playbook
- **Top of Funnel**: UGC videos (15–30 sec), testimonials, problem-awareness content
- **Middle of Funnel**: Collection retargeting, value-based lookalikes
- **Bottom of Funnel**: Dynamic Product Ads (DPA), cart abandonment, offer-led creatives

### Google Ads Playbook
- Shopping campaigns with optimised product feeds
- Branded search protection
- PMAX campaigns for broad discovery
- YouTube retargeting for high-AOV products

**Budget split we recommend:**
- 60% Meta, 30% Google, 10% testing new channels

## 5. Retention & Automation

Most brands leave 20–30% of recoverable revenue on the table by ignoring retention.

**Must-have automated flows:**
1. **Welcome Series** (3 emails over 5 days)
2. **Abandoned Cart** (Email + WhatsApp within 1 hour)
3. **Post-Purchase** (Upsell + Review request + Referral)
4. **Win-back** (Lapsed customers at 60/90/120 days)

We typically see **15–25% of total revenue** coming from automated email/WhatsApp flows for our clients.
