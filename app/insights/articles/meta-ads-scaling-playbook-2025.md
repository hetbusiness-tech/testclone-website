## Scaling Meta Ads in the Era of AI & Automation

In 2025, manual audience hacking and micro-interest targeting are obsolete. Meta's Andromeda algorithm favors accounts with **creative diversity, clean account structures, and broad targeting**.

Here is our agency framework for scaling daily ad spend reliably.

---

## 1. Account Architecture for High-Spend D2C Accounts

We simplify our account structure into 3 core campaigns:

1. **Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (ASC)**: Takes 70% of acquisition budget. Leverages Meta's machine learning across the entire catalog.
2. **Creative Testing Campaign (CBO / ABO)**: Dedicated budget for testing 8–12 new creative angles weekly.
3. **High-Value Retargeting / Retention**: Dynamic Product Ads targeting cart abandoners within 7 days.

---

## 2. Creative Velocity Framework

| Creative Format | Optimal Hook Duration | Best Use Case | Expected CTR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UGC Unboxing / Review** | First 2 seconds | Top of Funnel / Social Proof | 2.5% – 3.8% |
| **Problem vs Solution Reel** | First 1.5 seconds | Objection handling | 2.0% – 3.2% |
| **Founder Story / BTS** | First 3 seconds | Brand building & High AOV | 1.8% – 2.6% |
| **Static 3-Feature Carousel** | Instant visual | Retargeting & Catalog sales | 3.0% – 4.5% |

---

## 3. Scaling Rules

- **Increase Budget by 20% Every 48 Hours**: Avoid triggering significant algorithmic re-learning.
- **Kill Losers Fast**: If a creative doesn't hit target CAC within 3x CPA threshold, turn it off.
- **Double Down on Creative Iterations**: When a winner is found, create 5 hook variations of that exact same video.
